FAQ

Common Questions

What happens during a reading?

You share the questions you'd like to explore, and Acharya Ji walks you through your reading and answers your questions.

What details do I need?

Acharya Ji will tell you what's needed when you get in touch. For a compatibility reading, only share another person's details with their agreement.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the type and length of session and are confirmed clearly before booking. Nothing is charged until you agree.

Is my information private?

Sessions are private. This website uses analytics cookies; personal details you share are never sold and can be deleted on request at astroguruju698@gmail.com.

Is a reading a substitute for professional advice?

No. Readings are for personal reflection. For legal, medical, financial or mental-health matters, please speak with a qualified professional.
