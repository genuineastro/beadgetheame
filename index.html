FAQ

Common Questions

What happens in a coaching session?

You talk through your situation, and Acharya Ji helps you identify the patterns behind it, think through your options, and plan practical next steps.

Can my partner join?

Yes. Sessions can be one-to-one or with both partners, whichever you're more comfortable with.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the length and number of sessions and are confirmed clearly before booking. Nothing is charged until you agree.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is practical, forward-looking support and is not a substitute for therapy, medical, legal or financial advice. If you're experiencing abuse or feel unsafe, please contact your local emergency services or a support service in your area.

Is my information private?

Sessions are private. This website uses analytics cookies; personal details you share are never sold and can be deleted on request at astroguruju698@gmail.com.
